Research programme · Evaluation science

Beyond the Benchmark

Dynamic and distribution-based evaluation in NLP — from stochastic paraphrasing, to multidimensional robustness, to Measurement-as-Distribution.

This programme asks what we learn when evaluation is treated as a measurement process rather than a single fixed score. The work makes variation, uncertainty and robustness visible through controlled generation, perturbation and repeated evaluation.

Measurement-as-Distribution

The proposed protocol represents an evaluation as P = (C, G, Φ, E, R): construct specification, data generation, validated perturbation, repeated evaluation, and reporting.

Construct

Specify the capability and the claim the evaluation is intended to support.

Generate

Create evaluation evidence through a principled and reproducible process.

Perturb

Introduce validated variation while preserving the intended construct.

Evaluate

Repeat evaluation so uncertainty and variability become observable.

Report

Report distributions and stability alongside point estimates.

What the work examines

Score versus rank

A model can retain its relative ordering while its absolute score changes under valid perturbation.

Multiple robustness axes

Lexical/stylistic, length and language variation can produce partly decoupled robustness profiles.

Evaluation as evidence

When inputs and conditions vary, the distribution of measurements becomes part of the evidence.
